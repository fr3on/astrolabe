# Notice on source texts

Files named `source-*.txt` under `tasks/`, and the `reference` field in `data/exact-text/questions.jsonl`, contain the text of
public Saudi and Egyptian legal sources (statutes, decrees and Council of State fatwas). They come from the Dike legal corpus
(https://dike.it.com). They are included so the benchmark can be run, and they keep the terms of their original publishers and
of Dike. They are not covered by the CC BY 4.0 licence on our own work.

The client facts in each task are invented. They describe no real person or case.
